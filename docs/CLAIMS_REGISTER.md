# Claims register

This register separates validated evidence from development work and roadmap language. It is an editorial guardrail, not a substitute for the underlying paper and test documentation.

| Topic | Public wording used | Evidence level / constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal performance | Up to 16 °C lower surface temperature; 15.7 °C average difference in the displayed measurement | Measured on the Munich demonstrator against an adjacent flat reference wall in August 2025. Attribute the effect to the façade system and geometry; do not convert it into HVAC-energy or city-scale UHI savings. |
| Demonstrator | 12 m² full-scale demonstrator with 85 printed ceramic elements, installed in 2025 | Built and functional demonstrator. Do not describe it as certified or production-ready. |
| Recycled content | 40 wt% recycled ceramic content in the dry material mix | Applies to the published material formulation and the demonstrator recipe. Do not generalize to total life-cycle impact. |
| Flexural strength | 11.2 N/mm² average parallel to print layers versus an 8 N/mm² conventional tile reference | Initial three-point bending tests adapted from DIN EN 14411 / DIN EN ISO 10545-4. Not a certification result. |
| Habitat functions | Designed to provide nesting and shelter opportunities; bat shelter shown as a functional printed prototype | No occupancy monitoring at the pilot due to privacy restrictions. Never claim verified use, biodiversity gain or ecological impact. |
| Certification | Certification pathway; material tests and FEM-based assessment underway | No certification or building-regulatory approval has been obtained yet. Avoid completion dates. |
| FEM and simulation | Prototypes / tools toward approval-oriented verification | Do not call them certified or fully validated workflows. |
| Traction | Six non-binding Letters of Intent | Do not name organizations without explicit approval and do not imply contracts or revenue. |
| Commercial pilot | First paid 100 m² pilot | Roadmap objective, not current traction. |
| Patent | A patent process relating to the rotating rectangular nozzle is underway; final status wording remains subject to confirmation | Temporary public placeholder based on the team’s current information. Exact procedural wording must be approved by Julia or patent counsel before launch. Do not say patented, granted or nearly granted. |
| DGNB / LEED | Potential contribution to project assessment | No automatic credit or certification contribution is promised. Project-specific assessment is required. |
| Longevity | Omitted | The discussed 100-year statement is not sufficiently evidenced for publication. |
