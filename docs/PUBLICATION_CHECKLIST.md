# Publication checklist

The site is technically deployable, but the following items should be resolved before public launch.

## Required

- [ ] Add approved founder portraits and confirm their credits.
- [ ] Confirm Julia Larikova’s, Niklas Ebert’s, Martin Slepicka’s and Alexander Haynack’s exact public role wording.
- [ ] Confirm advisor names, academic titles and permission to list them.
- [ ] Complete a legally reviewed German **Impressum**. Because BioLope is not yet an incorporated company, identify the responsible natural person or institution and provide the required service address and contact details. Do not invent these details.
- [ ] Complete a privacy notice. The current implementation has no analytics, cookies, forms or third-party embeds; reassess if any of those are added.
- [ ] Obtain a final internal publication approval for the six-LoI statement and all named ecosystem organizations.
- [ ] Verify image publication rights and the credits in `docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md`.

## Recommended

- [ ] Confirm whether the patent process may be mentioned and approve the exact legal wording.
- [ ] Decide whether to add the final logo and brand font once available.
- [ ] Confirm the final domain, then configure GitHub Pages DNS and add its `CNAME`.
- [ ] Run a final link, keyboard, mobile, performance and reduced-motion check after content is locked.
- [ ] Recheck the preprint status shortly before launch; update “under review” if publication status changes.

No placeholder legal language is rendered publicly. This avoids presenting incomplete or fabricated operator information as a valid legal notice.
